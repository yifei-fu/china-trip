五城详The route

Movement I · Days 1–2

Shanghai

上海 · 沪 2 nights · the soft coast

A late-afternoon landing, the Bund as the Pudong towers come awake across the river, a hotel near the train you'll catch the day after next.

The Bund from above · 外滩
Pudong across the river · 浦东
Yu Garden at opening · 豫园
Xiaolongbao · 小笼包

Walk 行

Bund south-to-north at dusk for the skyline waking up. Yu Garden at opening, before the tour buses. Plane-trees and lane houses along Anfu and Wulumuqi in the former French Concession.

Eat 食

Xiaolongbao at Jia Jia Tang Bao. Shengjianbao at Yang's, eaten standing. Scallion-oil noodles, red-braised pork (hong shao rou), and cong you bing pancakes from a corner stall for breakfast. Heat: low — Shanghai is the soft palate.

Fun 玩

Rooftop drinks at Bar Rouge or Vue. Speakeasies tucked behind plane-trees on Yongkang Lu. M50 art district for the warehouses. Late-night Cantonese karaoke if energy returns.

Stay 宿

Near People's Square or West Nanjing Rd — Metro lines 2 / 10 are direct to Hongqiao for the train out.

Hongqiao → Zhangjiajie West → HSR ~6h via Changsha
Movement II · Days 3–5

Zhangjiajie

张家界 3 nights · where the dragon hides

Sandstone pillars in cloud, three thousand of them, the landscape Cameron scouted before drawing Pandora.

Yuanjiajie pillars · 袁家界
Tianmen Cave · 天门洞
Cliffside stairs · 鬼谷栈道
Yellow Stone Village · 黄石寨

Walk 行

Yuanjiajie via Bailong Elevator at first slot — the Avatar pillars before the crowds. Tianzi the same afternoon. Tianmen Mountain the next day: cable car up, glass skywalk along the cliff, 99 bends down through the cave.

Eat 食

Tujia mountain cooking — sour fish (酸鱼) braised with chillies, smoke-cured bacon stir-fried with dried mustard greens, sanxiaguo three-layer hotpot. Stalls in Wulingyuan town do glutinous-rice cakes for breakfast.

Fun 玩

Zhangjiajie Grand Canyon glass bridge — 430 m span, 300 m up, walk across if vertigo allows. Tianmen Fox Fairy show at night, projected on the mountainside. Cliffside plank trails for the brave.

Stay 宿

Two nights Wulingyuan East Gate (inside the park area), one in Zhangjiajie city for the Tianmen day and the morning train out.

Zhangjiajie West → Xi'an North → HSR ~7h via Wuhan
Movement III · Days 6–8

Xi'an

西安 · 长安 3 nights · the buried capital

Once Chang'an — capital of thirteen dynasties, the eastern terminus of the Silk Road. The walls are still here. The clay armies are still here. At night the Tang district lights up like the dynasty never ended.

Datang Everlasting Fame · 大唐不夜城
Terracotta Army · 兵马俑
Ming city wall · 城墙
Biang-biang noodles · 𰻞𰻞面

Walk 行

Terracotta Army in Lintong — Pit 3 → 2 → 1, the scale builds toward you. Ming wall by bike at sunset from South Gate, 14 km loop. Muslim Quarter alleys after dark. Datang Everlasting Fame after 8 pm for the lit-up Tang facades.

Eat 食

Biang-biang noodles — that one impossible character. Paomo at Lao Sun Jia, broken bread in lamb broth. Roujiamo, the Chinese burger. Liangpi cold noodles, persimmon doughnuts, glutinous rice with rose syrup from the Muslim Quarter stalls.

Fun 玩

Rent a Tang-dynasty hanfu in the Datang district and walk among the lit halls — hair and makeup included, it's the local thing to do. Shaanxi History Museum first hour for the bronze and the Tang tri-colour glaze. Optional: Mt. Hua day-trip for the plank walk.

Stay 宿

Inside the city wall, near Bell Tower — walk to everything except Terracotta and Datang (both an easy taxi or metro).

Xi'an North → Chengdu East → HSR ~3h 30m
Movement IV · Days 9–10

Chengdu

成都 · 蓉 2 nights · the slowest pulse

Pandas at dawn, opera at dusk, teahouses where afternoons disappear. Also: the queer capital of China — the densest queer nightlife in the country, gathered around Taikoo Li and Jiuyanqiao.

Pandas at dawn · 大熊猫
Anshun Bridge · 安顺廊桥
Heming Teahouse · 鹤鸣茶社
Bobo chicken · 钵钵鸡

Walk 行

Panda Base at 7:30 — nursery first, before the bamboo nap. Sichuan opera at Shufeng Yayun, front rows for the bian lian. Heming Teahouse in People's Park before 9 a.m. on a weekday — bamboo chairs, gaiwan tea, ear-cleaners doing the rounds. Wenshu Monastery for a quieter afternoon.

Eat 食

The first hotpot — mild, calibration for Chongqing. Yuanyang split-pot with weila on the spicy side. Mapo tofu at Chen Mapo, dandan noodles, fuqi feipian (sliced beef and tripe in chilli oil), sweet-water noodles, and "rabbit head" if curiosity wins.

Fun 玩

Jiuyanqiao bar street along the Jin River — Chengdu's Lan Kwai Fong, loud and cheap. Taikoo Li for queer-friendly bars and clubs (the scene is dense, easy to find). Mahjong in any tea garden if a local invites.

Stay 宿

Chunxi Road / Taikoo Li. Line 3 direct to pandas, Line 2 to opera, nightlife underfoot.

Chengdu East → Chongqing North → HSR ~1h 17m
Movement V · Days 11–13

Chongqing

重庆 · 渝 3 nights · the finale

Roads on the eleventh floor, trains through buildings, cableways across the Yangtze. On the night of day 12, Hongyadong lights gold against the Jialing. The last image is engineered.

Hongyadong from Qiansimen Bridge · 洪崖洞
Light rail at Liziba · 李子坝
Yangtze cableway · 长江索道
Chongqing hotpot · 火锅

Walk 行

Liziba on the arrival afternoon — the Metro Line 2 train threads through floors 6–8 of an apartment tower; watch from the riverside viewing platform. Yangtze cableway at golden hour. Qiansimen Bridge twenty minutes before Hongyadong's lights come on, around 7:30 pm.

Eat 食

The final hotpot — volcanic, oilier, more numbing than Chengdu's. Jiugongge nine-square pot or yuanyang with weila. Chongqing xiaomian for breakfast — chilli-oil noodles, 6 RMB a bowl. Suanlafen sweet-potato glass noodles in sour-spicy broth.

Fun 玩

The "8D magic city" routes — buildings where your ground-floor exit lands twenty storeys above your entrance (Kuixinglou is the famous one). Hongyadong from the inside, eleven levels stacked into a cliff. Yangtze night cruise if the second evening allows. Foot massage in a Jiefangbei mall to recover from the hotpot.

Stay / Exit 宿 · 归

Jiefangbei CBD. Metro Line 10 to CKG. CKG → London via PEK, PVG, DOH, or AMS.

估价The cost

Rough per-person estimate, in pounds, for a group of six sharing apartment rentals or three-star rooms. Economy flights, mostly local food. Yuan converted at ~9 RMB/£.

£700–900
London → Shanghai out, Chongqing → London back. Open-jaw, economy. BA, Cathay, Air China, China Eastern.
HSR · 4 legs 高铁
£170–200
Second class throughout. The two long legs (Shanghai → Zhangjiajie and Zhangjiajie → Xi'an) are ~£60 each; the two short legs are under £30. Book on 12306 once the 15-day window opens.
Lodging · 13 nights 住宿
£400–580
Comfortable mid-range hotels or boutique apartments — two per room, each with their own bed. ~£30–45 per head per night. Cheapest in Zhangjiajie and Xi'an; dearest in Shanghai. Local platforms (Trip.com, Tujia) beat international ones for inventory.

£350–500
~£25–35/day. Street food at ¥30–50 a meal, hotpot dinners at ¥150–250, the occasional ¥400 splurge.

事务Logistics

Visa 签证

UK passports enter visa-free 30 days, in force through 31 Dec 2026. Re-check by mid-Feb 2027. Plan B is the L-visa via CVASC, ~£100–170, 2–4 weeks.

Payments 支付

Link Visa or Mastercard directly to Alipay and WeChat Pay. Pre-load Revolut or Wise. Test ≥7 days before flying. Carry ¥2,000–3,000 cash for the stalls.

Trains 高铁

Register on 12306 ≥30 days out. Booking window opens 15 days before each train at 08:00 Beijing. Book all four legs in one transaction for adjacent seats.

Connect 网络

An eSIM (Holafly or Nomad) bypasses the GFW without a VPN. Astrill as primary VPN, Mullvad as backup. Apple Maps offline.
